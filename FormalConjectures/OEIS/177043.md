# Proof Sketch

The conjecture states that two formulas for the central MacMahon numbers $a(n)$ are equal:
1. The direct formula: $a(n) = \sum_{i=0}^n (-1)^{n-i} \binom{2n+1}{n-i} (2i+1)^{2n}$.
2. The Zhang formula: $z(n) = (-1)^n D(2n+1)$, where $D(q)$ is a double sum involving Stirling numbers of the second kind.

To prove this, we first simplify the direct formula. By reversing the index and shifting, we can rewrite it as:
$$ a(n) = \sum_{l=0}^n (-1)^l \binom{2n+1}{l} (2n+1-2l)^{2n} $$

Next, we relate the term $(2n+1-2l)^{2n}$ to the function $E(j_r, j_s, 2n)$ using the identity:
$$ \sum_{j=0}^m S(m, j) j! \binom{x}{j} = x^m $$
Applying this to $E(j_r, j_s, 2n)$, we can show that:
$$ \sum_{j_r} \sum_{j_s} E(j_r, j_s, 2n) \binom{x}{j_r} \binom{y}{j_s} = (x-y)^{2n} $$
Setting $x = 2n+1-l$ and $y = l$, we get:
$$ (2n+1-2l)^{2n} = \sum_{j_r} \sum_{j_s} E(j_r, j_s, 2n) \binom{2n+1-l}{j_r} \binom{l}{j_s} $$

Substituting this back into the expression for $a(n)$ and interchanging the sums, we get a sum over $j_r, j_s$ of $E(j_r, j_s, 2n)$ multiplied by an inner sum over $l$:
$$ \sum_{l=0}^n (-1)^l \binom{2n+1}{l} \binom{2n+1-l}{j_r} \binom{l}{j_s} $$
By manipulating the binomial coefficients and evaluating the alternating sum over $l$, we show that this inner sum exactly matches the coefficient of $E(j_r, j_s, 2n)$ in the Zhang formula $z(n)$. This establishes $z(n) = a(n)$.

# Formal Proof

The formal proof in `Solution.lean` proceeds by:
1. `sum_alternating_choose_partial` and `sum_alternating_choose`: Basic alternating sums of binomial coefficients.
2. `sum_stirling_choose`: Proves $\sum_{j=0}^n S(n, j) j! \binom{x}{j} = x^n$ by induction on $n$.
3. `sum_E_choose`: Uses `sum_stirling_choose` to evaluate the double sum involving $E(j_r, j_s, 2n)$ and binomial coefficients, reducing it to $\sum_r (-1)^{2n-r} \binom{2n}{r} x^r y^{2n-r}$.
4. `sum_E_choose_eq_pow`: Uses the binomial theorem to show the above sum equals $(x-y)^{2n}$.
5. `zhang_eq_a`:
   - Expands the Zhang formula.
   - Rewrites the direct formula $a(n)$ as $\sum_{l=0}^n (-1)^l \binom{2n+1}{l} (2n+1-2l)^{2n}$.
   - Uses `sum_E_choose_eq_pow` to express $(2n+1-2l)^{2n}$ as a double sum of $E(j_r, j_s, 2n) \binom{2n+1-l}{j_r} \binom{l}{j_s}$.
   - Interchanges the sums.
   - Evaluates the inner sum over $l$ using `sum_coef` and `inner_sum_zero`, showing it matches the coefficients in the Zhang formula.
